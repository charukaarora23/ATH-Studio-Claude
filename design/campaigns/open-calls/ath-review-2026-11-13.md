Partial review — Chrome is not available in this session and Instagram is blocked from here, so I did not look at the live accounts today. ## Verdict

The launch carousel now looks like a publisher, not a contest — but the thing that makes this model legitimate is still the weakest part of the post: **proof that being in the book gets an artist seen.** Two real photos and two real past artists will do more for conversion than any further design change. Until they are in, the post will keep reading as "pay to enter, pay to buy".

## What's broken

**1. The proof slots are empty (slides 2, 5, 10).** The carousel promises a hardcover book and introductions to galleries, and shows neither a book nor a single published artist. The ATH business has a reputational edge — "pay to enter, then pay to buy the book you're in" has a reading that ends the brand. The only defence is visible proof of exposure. Today the post asks for trust it doesn't earn.

**2. The launch carousel was pitching the purchase.** "Copies for you — available at a subsidised rate" sat in the benefits stack. On a launch post aimed at cold artists, that line is exactly the reading we must avoid. *Fixed today:* removed from the carousel (it belongs in the brief the DM sends). "Introductions to collectors, galleries and curators" now sits at the top of the stack, where the eye lands first.

**3. The reach number undersells the asset.** The carousel says 190K+ followers. The verified context (13 July) puts the two Instagram accounts at **~382,000** combined — @artstoheartsproject 188K + @opencallforartists_ 194K — plus 100K+ email. If the call is promoted on both accounts, "382K artists see this book's campaign" is a stronger and still honest line. *Not changed* — Rabia to confirm both accounts promote the book before it goes on a slide.

**4. Nothing stopped a stale post going out.** The 13 July audit found captions with deadlines that had already passed. *Fixed today in Post Studio:* saving a PNG or the ZIP now warns if the deadline has passed or if any dashed (missing) detail is still on a slide.

**5. The caption was written separately from the post.** That is how duplicate and stale captions happen. *Fixed today:* Post Studio now writes the caption and the pinned comment from the same call details as the slides, so they always match the keyword and the deadline.

**6. The DM keyword is right for now, wrong after ~8 December.** The comment-trigger automation is restricted until about 8 December. The call closes 13 December. The last five days are the highest-intent days of the month — they should run on a comment trigger (*COMMENT "GARDEN"*), which also lifts reach.

## The plan

- **This week:** get the four real assets in (books together, an open spread, two past artists with permission). Publish the launch only when the dashed boxes are gone.
- **Through the month:** every Garden post that is *editorial* — featured artists, the theme, "inside the book" — must not end in a submission pitch. Give those posts their own keyword that delivers more art, not an application link (see How).
- **From ~8 December:** switch every Garden CTA from "DM GARDEN" to "COMMENT GARDEN" for the final countdown.

## How

- **Proof:** slide 2 = one photo of the editions together. Slide 5 = one open spread with a real artist's name, title, medium, country. Slide 10 = two artists: name, edition, country, one line each, in their own words.
- **Editorial capture for the Garden month** — the one that delivers art, not a submission call:
  - CTA on the post: *COMMENT "GARDENLIST" and we'll send you the 12 garden artworks we're loving this month.*
  - The DM reply sends the list — **not** the application link. Those people go on a separate list (art lovers / collectors).
- **Final week, after the automation returns:** *COMMENT "GARDEN" — we'll DM you the brief and your link. Closes 13 December.*
