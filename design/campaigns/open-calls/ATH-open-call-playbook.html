Arts to Hearts · Open calls

Every call, 30 days, 15 posts.

The operating plan for running three open calls at once. Each call runs 30 days and gets 15 feed posts, a story every day and a DM sequence. Each call takes an artist through the same steps in the same order, so the feed teaches people how ATH calls work. A new call launches every 10 days. That means one call is always launching, one is building trust and one is closing.

1 · See

"Calling all ___ artists." They recognise themselves in the first line.

2 · Comment

One keyword. A small, free yes that costs nothing.

3 · DM

Brief, link and a free guide arrive at once. We now have them in DMs.

4 · Start

"One artwork is enough." Make the first step feel small.

5 · Submit

Reminders at 10 days, 3 days and the last day, by post, story and DM.

Every post moves people one step down this ladder. The keyword is the hinge: once someone comments, they're in the DM list and get the reminders even if they miss the posts.

The psychology

Four phases, four questions in the artist's head

Days 1–10

Attention

"Is this for me?"

Identity and self-relevance. Name the artist, show the theme is wide, make the book feel real.

  • Identity call-out
  • Self-relevance
  • Tangible proof (the book)
  • Value anchor

Days 11–20

Trust

"Is this legit, and is it worth it?"

Show the people behind it. Past artists, the curator, real numbers. Answer the doubts before they're asked.

  • Social proof
  • Authority
  • Objection handling
  • Reciprocity (free guide)

Days 21–27

Momentum

"Are others doing this?"

Real entry numbers and real dates. Others are already in, and the window is closing.

  • Momentum
  • Real scarcity (101 places)
  • Commitment reminder

Days 28–30

Urgency

"If not now, never."

Fear of missing out, with true dates only. Every channel at once: post, story, DM, ad.

  • Loss aversion
  • Deadline
  • One last easy step
Rule that keeps it honest: every number and date is real. No countdown clocks, no "only 5 spots left". 101 places and a fixed deadline are real scarcity, and that's enough.

One call, start to finish

The 15 feed posts, in order

Call-day 1 is launch day and call-day 30 is the deadline. Posts fall on set days so they fit with the other two calls (see the calendar below).

DayPhasePostFormatWhy it converts

Mix per call: 7 carousels, 6 singles, 1 reel, 1 combined post. Every post ends on the call's keyword.

Three calls at once

Launch every 10 days. Never collide.

With launches 10 days apart, one call is always new, one is in the middle and one is closing. Each 10-day block repeats the same pattern: the new call posts on odd days, the middle call on even days, and the closing call on days 1, 4, 7, 9 and 10. That's 15 posts every 10 days, about one or two a day, and no call ever posts twice in a row.

Two posts on one day

The closing call posts in the morning, the other call in the evening. Urgency gets the best slot.

All calls together

Day 24 of every call is a combined "Closing soon" post listing every open call. The same list runs as a pinned story highlight called "Open now".

Each call stays recognisable

Each call keeps one artwork, one name, one keyword and one hook for all 30 days. The frame and templates never change.

Grid check

Before scheduling, check the grid. The countdowns look the same across calls, so make sure two never sit side by side.

Every day, outside the feed

Stories, DMs and ads do the reminding

Stories, daily

One card per open call each day, with the keyword and a link sticker. Add the native Instagram countdown sticker on launch day so people can tap "remind me". Rotate a poll, a Q&A box, reposts of entries and a book clip.

DM sequence

On comment: brief, link and free guide. Day 21: "10 days left, here's your link." Day 27: "3 days left." Day 30 morning: "Closes tonight." Everyone who commented gets these, even if they never see the posts.

Ads follow the phases

Days 1–10: the "Calling all ___ artists" cover, your best hook at ₹212 per application. Days 11–20: proof and value, optimised for sign-ups. Days 21–30: "Few days to apply" plus retargeting with "finish your entry".

Caption formula

Line 1: the identity hook and the keyword. Then what you get, the proof, who can apply, the deadline, and the keyword again. Keep it under 120 words.
