Post 1 · Today, 13 Nov · 30 days left

Launch carousel, slide by slide

Written like a publisher calling for work, not a contest. The order is the publication, its track record, the curatorial idea, how selection works, what's inside the book, what it means for an artist's career, the awards, and how to submit. Selectivity is what gives selection its value.

Slide 1 · Cover

Submissions open. 101 Art Book: Garden Edition.

"A juried, hardcover publication of 101 contemporary artists. Submissions close 13 December." The Garden painting in a frame. Countdown strip.

Reads as a publication calling for work, not a contest. "Juried" and "submissions" carry professional weight.

Slide 2 · The series

The sixteenth edition of the 101 Art Book series.

"Since 2023, the series has published 1,300+ artists across 15 editions. Past cover artists include Ian Davenport (Colors Edition) and Tana Gaxiola (Nature Edition)." A row of past book spines.

Track record and recognised names set the level.

Slide 3 · The theme

"A garden is more than a place where things grow."

A short curatorial statement from the call page: a garden as colour, form, memory, growth and decay. "We want to see how you interpret it."

The curatorial voice shows there's a point of view behind the book.

Slide 4 · Selection

How work is selected

"Every submission is reviewed by our jury, led by [guest curator]. We look for originality, a strong interpretation of the theme and the quality of the work. 101 artists are selected. Results within three weeks of the deadline."

Selectivity is the opposite of "anyone can come". It's what gives selection its value.

Slide 5 · Inside the edition

More than a catalogue

"Alongside the 101 artists: interviews with our guest curator, collectors, galleries and the cover artist. A premium hardcover with gold-foil detailing." Photos of a past edition's spreads.

Editorial depth makes it a book people collect, not a directory.

Slide 6 · For selected artists

A publication credit for your practice

A published credit for your CV, website and grant applications. A Certificate of Recognition. Distribution through Amazon, Barnes & Noble and independent bookstores. Introduction to our network of collectors, curators and galleries.

Frames the benefit as career value, not as freebies.

Slide 7 · Recognition

Three artists will receive additional recognition.

Winner: $500, a 4-page feature, a glass trophy and an Artsy listing. Two runners-up: a 2-page feature and a glass trophy.

The prize comes last and is framed as recognition from the jury, not a giveaway.

Slide 8 · Submit

Submit your work by 13 December.

"Open to artists aged 18+ worldwide, in all media. Submit through the link in our bio. For the full brief, DM us GARDEN."

A calm, direct call. Eligibility stated factually, not as "anyone can".

Tone

Sound like a publisher, not a contest

Use

  • Submissions, submit
  • Juried, selected, the jury
  • Publication, edition, series
  • Recognition, award
  • Publication credit, professional record

Avoid

  • "Anyone can apply", "no degree needed"
  • "Win $500" as a headline
  • "Don't miss out", "last chance" shouting
  • Strings of emojis, exclamation marks
  • Prize before publication

Where eligibility goes

One factual line on the last slide: "Open to artists 18+ worldwide, in all media." Questions about being self-taught or unpublished are answered in the FAQ posts as "the jury judges the work, not the CV". That's an answer, not a selling point.

Urgency, professionally

Use dates and process, not pressure: "Submissions close 13 December. The jury begins reviewing on the 14th." A deadline stated calmly by a serious publisher creates more urgency than shouting.

Every day

Stories and DMs

Daily Garden story

  • Frame 1, every day: the countdown card with the days left, a link sticker to apply, and "DM us GARDEN".
  • Frame 2, rotating: a poll ("Which garden is yours: wild or tended?"), a Q&A box, a repost of an artist sharing the call, a clip of a past book, or that day's feed post.
  • Launch day: add Instagram's countdown sticker set to 13 Dec, so people can tap "remind me".
  • Final week: three frames a day, and on 13 Dec, morning, afternoon and two hours before close.

Story highlight "Open now"

One cover per open call: Garden, Woman Artist Award, and the next new call. Update the days left daily. This is where someone lands from the bio.

Reminders, without sending DMs

  • Instagram broadcast channel "Open Calls": people opt in from the saved reply and the bio. Post there at 10 days, 7 days, 3 days, the day before and the last morning.
  • Countdown sticker on the launch-day story, so people tap "remind me" and Instagram notifies them.
  • Email: anyone who started an application on the website gets "finish your entry" emails at 7 days, 3 days and the last day.
  • Replies only: we answer DMs people send us and never start one, even after automation returns on 8 Dec.

Ads, in step

  • 13 to 22 Nov: the launch cover, "Calling all artists who paint nature".
  • 23 Nov to 2 Dec: the prize and what-you-get posts, for sign-ups.
  • 3 to 13 Dec: countdowns, plus retargeting with "finish your entry".

Needed to make it real

Jury or curator for Garden (27 Nov carousel, 8 Dec quote): name, photo, a line on what they look for.
Nature Edition artists (25 Nov carousel): permission and images from 4–5 artists, e.g. cover artist Tana Gaxiola, Michael Nauert, Elise Wehle, Noémie L. Côté, Sahaya Sharma (cash award winner).
Book footage (17 Nov reel): a flip-through of the Nature or Colors Edition.
Entry numbers (2 and 11 Dec): real counts and countries from the submissions dashboard.
Guest curator or jury for Garden (launch slide 4): the single most important piece for credibility.
Past edition spreads (launch slides 2 and 5): photos of real book spines and interior pages.
Testimonials (25 Nov carousel): 2–3 short quotes from past selected artists, with name, country and edition.
Free guide (in the saved reply and 1 Dec): "How to photograph your art for print". One page, based on the call page's practical note.
Entry fee, for the DM and FAQ.
