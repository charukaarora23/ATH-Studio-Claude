The countdown strip

A thin band at the bottom of every Garden post, so whichever post someone sees, they know it closes, when, and what to do. It also points to the other open calls, so no lead is wasted.

30 DAYS LEFT · Closes 13 Dec · Apply: link in bio · DM us "GARDEN" Also open: Artist of the Year (3 days) · Woman Artist Award (17 days)

The number updates on every post. Once Artist of the Year closes on 16 Nov, it drops off "Also open", and the next new call joins it.

Post 1 · Today, 13 Nov · 30 days left

Launch carousel, slide by slide

The order is direct call, then scale, belonging, proof, value, prize and doubts, then the call again. By slide 8 the artist knows what this is, that it's real, that people like them get in, and exactly what to do.

Slide 1 · Direct call

Open call: get published in our Garden Edition.

"Calling all artists who paint nature. 101 places in a hardcover art book. Win $500. Closes 13 December." The Garden painting in a frame, plus the countdown strip.

Says exactly what it is and what to do, in the first second. It opens with your best ad hook.

Slide 2 · Scale

16 books. 1,300+ artists. One growing collection.

"Since 2023, the 101 Art Book series has published 1,300+ artists in 15 editions, sold on Amazon and Barnes & Noble, and shared with our 190,000+ community." A row of past book spines.

Brand elevation: this is an established series, not a one-off.

Slide 3 · Belonging

Your name, beside 100 artists from around the world.

A spread from a past book with one page marked "Your work here". "Each edition becomes a record of how artists across the world see one theme."

Makes them feel this is something to be part of.

Slide 4 · Proof

Artists who've been in our books say it best.

Two or three short quotes from past selected artists, each with their name, country and edition. "Rated 4.9 by artists on Google."

Social proof from people like them, plus a public rating.

Slide 5 · What you get

If you're selected

Your work in print. A Certificate of Recognition. Features to 190,000+ followers and 100,000+ email readers. Sold on Amazon and Barnes & Noble.

Concrete value, before the ask.

Slide 6 · The prize

$500 and a 4-page feature.

For the winner. Glass trophies for the top three, plus an Artsy listing.

Money hooks drew 426 comments on the Creative Fund post.

Slide 7 · Doubts

"But I'm self-taught." You can apply.

"Never been published? Abstract work? Outside the US? Only one artwork? All welcome. No degree, gallery or exhibition history needed. Original work only, no AI."

Answers the three doubts that stop most artists.

Slide 8 · Direct call

Apply now. 101 places. 30 days.

"Apply through the link in our bio. Want the brief first? DM us GARDEN." Deadline: 13 December.

Two clear actions: the artist chooses to apply or to start the conversation.

Every day

Stories and DMs

Daily Garden story

  • Frame 1, every day: the countdown card with the days left, a link sticker to apply, and "DM us GARDEN".
  • Frame 2, rotating: a poll ("Which garden is yours: wild or tended?"), a Q&A box, a repost of an artist sharing the call, a clip of a past book, or that day's feed post.
  • Launch day: add Instagram's countdown sticker set to 13 Dec, so people can tap "remind me".
  • Final week: three frames a day, and on 13 Dec, morning, afternoon and two hours before close.

Story highlight "Open now"

One cover per open call: Garden, Woman Artist Award, and the next new call. Update the days left daily. This is where someone lands from the bio.

Reminders, without sending DMs

  • Instagram broadcast channel "Open Calls": people opt in from the saved reply and the bio. Post there at 10 days, 7 days, 3 days, the day before and the last morning.
  • Countdown sticker on the launch-day story, so people tap "remind me" and Instagram notifies them.
  • Email: anyone who started an application on the website gets "finish your entry" emails at 7 days, 3 days and the last day.
  • Replies only: we answer DMs people send us and never start one, even after automation returns on 8 Dec.

Ads, in step

  • 13 to 22 Nov: the launch cover, "Calling all artists who paint nature".
  • 23 Nov to 2 Dec: the prize and what-you-get posts, for sign-ups.
  • 3 to 13 Dec: countdowns, plus retargeting with "finish your entry".

Needed to make it real

Jury or curator for Garden (27 Nov carousel, 8 Dec quote): name, photo, a line on what they look for.
Nature Edition artists (25 Nov carousel): permission and images from 4–5 artists, e.g. cover artist Tana Gaxiola, Michael Nauert, Elise Wehle, Noémie L. Côté, Sahaya Sharma (cash award winner).
Book footage (17 Nov reel): a flip-through of the Nature or Colors Edition.
Entry numbers (2 and 11 Dec): real counts and countries from the submissions dashboard.
Testimonials for the launch, slide 4: 2–3 short quotes from past selected artists, with name, country and edition. This is the most important missing piece.
Free guide (in the saved reply and 1 Dec): "How to photograph your art for print". One page, based on the call page's practical note.
Entry fee, for the DM and FAQ.
