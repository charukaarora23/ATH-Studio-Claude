The idea

The call card is the brand.

One object turns up everywhere a call appears: the website listing, the last slide of every carousel, the story, the email header, the ad. It always shows the same things in the same order: art, status pill, series and edition, title, three fact tiles (deadline, artists, top award) and one Apply bar.

Someone who saw the card on Instagram finds the same card on the site. That match is what makes people trust the link and finish applying. Your web card references (photo on top, white panel, pills, two stat tiles, one strong button) are the model. The card now carries ATH type and colour.

Edition colours

Each call gets one pair.

Every pair comes from the ATH palette. Choose the pair that suits the theme's art, and keep it for the whole life of the call, from launch to the book. That way people learn the colour as well as the name.

Convert without hype

Six rules that make people trust the link.

Value before the ask

A carousel earns its Apply slide. Promise, theme and what you get come before the button.

Every claim checkable

Numbers come from the call page: 15 editions, 1,300+ artists, 190,000+ followers. Don't add followers and email subscribers together.

Real dates, real counts

"10 days left" only when it's true. No countdown clocks, no "spots filling fast". 101 is a real limit, so say it calmly.

State the cost

Hidden fees read as a scam. Put the entry fee on the call card and in the caption, the same way every time.

One action

One Apply bar per post. Saves and shares are the second action, never a second button.

Credit the art

Every artwork carries its artist's name. It's fair to artists, and it shows the people applying how they'll be treated.

30-day plan

One call, thirty days.

Each call gets about 13 feed posts and a story every day. With three calls running about ten days apart, the feed gets one or two posts a day and never shows the same call twice in a row. Day 30 is the deadline.

Key momentFeed postStories only
Garden Edition: launch around 12 Nov 2026, deadline 13 Dec 2026, so day 30 is 13 Dec. Results are due within three weeks of the deadline (by 3 Jan 2027).

Running three calls at once

Stagger them, never stack them.

  1. Launches

    Launch a new call about every 10 days. Never two launch carousels in the same week.

  2. Each day

    One feed post, chosen by priority: the call closest to its deadline comes first, then a launch, then the rest. Run stories for every open call each day, one card per call.

  3. Grid

    Edition colours make the grid read like a set of book spines. Check the grid view before scheduling so two posts in the same colour don't sit side by side.

  4. Link in bio

    Put one link page in the bio, with a call card for each open call, ordered by deadline. It's the same card again.

Before this goes live

Four things to confirm.

Entry fee. The call page doesn't show it unless you log in. Add it to the call card and the caption.
Artwork credits. The two portrait paintings came from the Creative Fund folder and still read "Artist Name". Add the artist and title, or swap in past Garden-style selected work.
Old carousel credits. In the earlier Garden posts, the Pissarro credit is on a Caillebotte painting and the Manet credit is on a Childe Hassam painting.
Jury and curator names. The page mentions a guest curator. Naming them is the strongest trust post in the plan.
