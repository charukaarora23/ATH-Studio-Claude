ATH Editions

The rules every Editions screen follows before it is redesigned: what the product is for, who it serves, and the colours, type, header, menus, drawers, footer and words it is built from. These sit under the Arts to Hearts Project design system and only add what Editions needs. Where the master system already decides something, this page points to it.

01 · The product

A shelf you can walk into

Editions is where ATH shows its published books: every series, every volume, and the artists inside them. In the master system's language it is the Shelf side of "Studio & Shelf": the collector's view of finished work.

WhoWhat they came to doThe one action we want

ArtistSee what being featured looks like and whether their work belongs.Submit for the next volume
Featured artistFind their page and share it.Share your page
Curator, pressFind artists and credits quickly.View artist

What Editions is not

  • Not the open-call site. It links to the next open call; it never runs the submission form itself.
  • Not a competition results page. Recognition (cover artist, winners) is shown as editorial credit, not as a podium.
  • Not a general blog. Every section is about a specific series, volume or artist.

How we'll know it works

  • Edition page: share of visitors who open the purchase drawer, then complete it.
  • Series page: share of visitors who open a volume.
  • Both: clicks on "Submit for Vol. 8" that reach the open-call page.

02 · Principles

Five rules that decide arguments

1

The book is the hero.

Real covers and real spreads lead every page. Type and colour frame the object; they never compete with it.

2

One job per screen, one primary button.

A screen exists to sell this edition, or to open a volume, or to show an artist. Everything else is a secondary button or a link.

3

Quiet register.

Editions speaks in the soft voice of the master system: Instrument Serif and Inter. The loud Archivo Black voice belongs to campaigns and open calls, not to the shelf.

4

Structure tells the truth.

Numbers, labels and breadcrumbs only appear when they mean something. A section number means a real sequence; a label names what is actually below it.

5

Honest and specific.

Real names, real prices, real page counts, real ship dates. No placeholder text ships, and no fake urgency.

03 · Current screens

What the guardrails fix

From the two screens you shared, the Studio Visit Book Vol. 7 page and the Studio Visit Books series page. Each problem becomes a rule.

Vol. 7 · sections

Section numbers run 01, 02, 04, 02, 03, 05.

Numbers repeat and skip, so they read as noise instead of order.

Number sections only on a real sequence. Product pages use named eyebrows without numbers.
Vol. 7 · labels

"Two ways to hold this collection" sits above "Selected artists".

The eyebrow describes a different section from the one below it.

An eyebrow names its own section in two to four words.
Vol. 7 · calls to action

Five competing calls to action.

Purchase $120, Be in the next one, Submit your work for Vol. 0, pink arrow pills, View all.

One primary per screen. Edition pages: "Buy this edition · $120". Everything else is secondary or a link.
Vol. 7 · content

Placeholder text and repeated names.

"Lorem ipsum" in the cover-artist quote; "Abigail Cruz" three times; "Vol. 0".

No screen ships with placeholder copy. Missing content hides its section.
Vol. 7 · recognition

A sports podium with 1st, 2nd and 3rd.

Gold rings and rank numbers turn a book into a contest.

Recognition is shown as editorial credit: "Cover artist", "Featured", with a portrait and one line.
Both · buttons

Pill buttons, square buttons and arrow chips mixed.

The master system says 12px corners and never pills.

Three button shapes only: primary, secondary, link. All 12px radius.
Both · dark bands

Three to four burgundy bands per page.

The hero, a ticker, a mid band and the footer all go dark, so nothing stands out.

At most one dark band between the header and the footer.
Both · navigation

The header links differ from the master site.

"The full story, Collections, Awards, Your journey, Your next 5 years, FAQ" against the master's "Open calls, Books, Artists, Magazine, Podcast, Shop".

One header across ATH. Editions lives under "Books". See decision 1.
Both · small text

Grey 11–12px body text in long paragraphs.

Much of the reading copy is tiny, centred and low-contrast.

Body 16px minimum, left-aligned, measure 68 characters, ink-700 or darker.

05 · Colour

Cream ground, burgundy lead

Only master-system tokens. Editions uses the light theme, follows the master 60 / 25 / 10 / 5 split, and keeps red for one small moment per page.

Rules

  • Each series may add one series colour from the master palette (for example sky for The Big Book). Studio Visit Books has none assigned yet: see decision 3.
  • Text on photography sits on a solid band or the master cover scrim, never directly on the image.
  • red-500 never appears in Editions UI. The pop is red-700, at most one per screen.
  • The dark burgundy in your current screens is close to burgundy-800; use the token, not a new value.

15 · Your decisions

Six things only you can settle

My recommendation is in bold. Everything else on this page follows from these.

1

Which header does the site use?

The current screens use "The full story, Collections, Awards, Your journey…". The master system uses "Open calls, Books, Artists, Magazine, Podcast, Shop". Recommend the master header, with Editions under Books.

2

Is Editions allowed the quiet register?

The master system pairs one loud font with one soft font in every piece. Editions would use Instrument Serif with Inter and no Archivo Black. Recommend yes, as a written exception for the Shelf side.

3

What is the Studio Visit Books series colour?

The master book table has no entry for this series. Recommend burgundy-500 with peony as its accent, matching what the current screens already lean towards.

4

Do winners stay on edition pages?

The podium suggests a contest. Recommend showing them as "Cover artist" and "Featured artists", with prizes on the Fine Art Prize pages.

5

Where does buying happen?

A purchase drawer on the edition page, or a jump to the Shop. Recommend the drawer, so the collector never leaves the book.

6

Do the My Studio colours move to the master tokens?

The My Studio prototype uses burgundy #6A0620 and chilli #AA0003; the master burgundy is #850020. Recommend moving My Studio to the master tokens so both products feel like one ATH.
