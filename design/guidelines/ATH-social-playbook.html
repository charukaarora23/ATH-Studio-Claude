01 · The five rules

Five rules before anything else

Every post, carousel, story and poster follows these. Everything else in this playbook is detail.

  1. One big thing per slide. A headline, a figure, a list or an artwork. Never two.
  2. Small things live in the corners. Series name, slide number, handle and arrow sit in the same four places on every slide.
  3. White and black carry, Burgundy signs. Most slides are white or black. Burgundy marks the logo, the badge, key covers and the end card.
  4. Two fonts do the work. Inter Tight for almost everything, Libre Baskerville for quotes and one italic phrase.
  5. The artwork is the hero. Never filter it, never cover the subject, always credit it.

Do

  • Two fonts per slide at most. The logo does not count.
  • Use italic Baskerville for one phrase, an artwork title or a name.

Don't

  • Use any font not listed here, including Canva defaults.

05 · Avoiding clutter

The count test

Before posting, count. If any number is over, split the slide in two or take something out.

On one slideAt most
Big things (headline, figure, list or artwork)1
Fonts2
Text sizes3
Accent colours1
Images1 (2 only for detail plus full view)
Calls to action1
Decorations: stickers, stars, hearts, scribbles, emoji0

Do

  • Leave at least a third of the slide empty.
  • Move details to the next slide or the caption.
  • Use rules (thin lines) to separate, not boxes and shadows.

Don't

  • Shadows, outlines or glows on text.
  • Coloured washes over artworks.
  • Centre text, except the serif and lockup layouts.

06 · Covers that convert

The cover decides whether anyone swipes

A good cover says what the reader gets, in ten words or fewer, in type big enough to read in the grid.

The question artists askIs a $35 entry fee worth it?
Name the readerFor artists who have never been published
Deadline + name3 days left: 101 Art Book: Faces
ResultThe 101 are in.

Type covers

For advice, guides and announcements. The arrow badge carries the signature colour.

Image covers

For open calls, features and anything where the art sells the post.

Do

  • Read the cover at thumbnail size before posting.
  • Put the reader's benefit in the headline, not in the caption.
  • Add a swipe cue on carousel covers.

Don't

  • Clickbait: "You won't believe…".
  • Covers with no headline at all, unless it is a pure artwork feature.
  • More than three lines of headline.

07 · Text on images

Put type where the painting is quiet

Find the calm area first, then place the text. If there is none, choose another layout or darken the whole image evenly.

SituationDo this
Light, empty areaDark type (Ink, Night, Burgundy) straight on it
Dark, calm areaWhite or Cream type straight on it
Busy everywhereDarken the whole image with black at 15 to 45%, or use the paper card
Facts to read (date, fee)Darkened base or the image-and-block layout

Do

  • Keep the subject's face and hands clear of text.
  • Check legibility on a phone at arm's length.

Don't

  • Coloured overlays (burgundy wash, pink tint).
  • Text over faces, signatures or the focal point.

08 · Featuring artwork

Show the work as the artist made it

Artists trust ATH with their work. Every feature should look like a museum label, not an advert.

Credit lineFormat
On the slideArtist Name, Title, year
On the feature slideMedium · size in cm
In the captionTag @artist in the first line; full credit; where to see more

Do

  • Show the whole work on at least one slide.
  • Colour-check against the artist's file.
  • Get written permission before posting.
  • Write alt text describing the work.

Don't

  • Filters, borders, mock-up frames or shadows.
  • Crops that cut a signature, face or the edge the artist chose.
  • Text over the work on a feature slide.

10 · Urgency

Escalate the colour as the deadline nears

Urgency only works if it is true and if it builds. The ground moves from White to Burgundy to Chilli, and the type gets bigger.

Do

  • Always give the date, the time and the time zone.
  • Repeat the web address on every urgency post.
  • Use the countdown sticker in stories.

Don't

  • Fake scarcity ("only 2 spots left") unless it is true.
  • Extending a deadline after a "last call" post without saying so.
  • Urgency colours on anything that is not a deadline.

11 · Numbers

Let the figure be the picture

A number on its own is a fact. A number with one line saying what it counts is a reason to apply.

Do

  • Use real, checked figures and give the source and date.
  • One figure per slide, or one list.
  • Set the unit (%, $, M) at about a third of the figure size.

Don't

  • Rounded-up or invented numbers.
  • Figures in Faculty Glyphic or Nord.
  • Charts on social unless the shape is the point.

The figures in these examples are placeholders.

12 · Participants

Celebrate every artist

Results posts are the ones artists share. Make every name readable and every artist findable.

Do

  • Spell names exactly as the artist does; check with them.
  • Alphabetical order by surname, always.
  • Tag every artist (20 tags per post at most; use more posts).
  • Get consent before showing a portrait.

Don't

  • Ranking artists or "top" lists unless it is a prize.
  • Cropping artworks to fit a grid tile beyond recognition.
  • Leaving anyone out of the names list.

Paintings and names shown are public-domain stand-ins.

13 · Captions and credits

The first line does the work

  • First line: what it is and the action.
  • Credit every artwork; tag the artist in the first line.
  • Five hashtags at most, at the end.
  • Alt text on every image.
  • The caption repeats the date, fee and link from the slides.

16 · Final check

Before you press post

  • One big thing on every slide; the count test passes.
  • Corners in place, same on every slide.
  • Only Inter Tight and Libre Baskerville; the logo is a placed file.
  • Surface is White, Black, Burgundy or Night; one accent at most.
  • Text is readable on a phone, nothing under 24 px.
  • Artwork uncropped, unfiltered, credited, artist tagged, permission on file.
  • Open call posts show book, deadline with time zone, fee and link.
  • Numbers are real and sourced.
  • Alt text written.
