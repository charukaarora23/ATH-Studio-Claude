01 · The logo

One name, five shapes

Every version is drawn from the real fonts: Faculty Glyphic for arts and hearts, Baskerville Italic for to, Nord for PROJECT. Always place the files. Never type the name out in a text box, because live text shifts the letters.

07 · Social

Flat colour, tight type, quiet corners

Every slide is one flat ground, one type colour and one thing to read. The small details (series name, slide number, handle, arrow) sit in the same four corners on every slide, so a carousel reads as one set and the middle of the slide stays clear.

The rules

  • One surface per slide: White, Black, Burgundy or Night. Pink for a single moment.
  • One big thing: a headline, a figure, a list or an image.
  • Everything else is small and sits in the corners.
  • 72 px margins on every side, every slide.
  • Type on images sits on the quiet areas, around the subject. Darken a busy image evenly if you must.

Never

  • Decorations: hearts, stars, sparkles, scribbles, stickers.
  • Shadows or outlines on text, coloured washes over images.
  • Text on top of a busy part of an image.
  • Secondary colours as backgrounds, or two accents on one slide.
  • Centred text, except the serif cover and the end card.

Covers

Same layout on the three surfaces. The badge carries the signature colour.

Text on images

Type sits directly on the artwork, placed around the subject, or in a clean block beside it. Where a painting is too busy, darken it evenly (black, 15 to 45%), never with a coloured wash.

Motion

Reels and animated stories: text arrives with a short rise and fade (about 0.25 s), cuts land on the beat, the arrow badge can turn once. End on a 2 second card: AtH or the name on Night, one line, the web address. No spinning, bouncing or glitter effects.

09 · Captions and credits

Artists first, every time

Credit line

  • Artist Name, Title, year · medium · size.
  • Tag the artist's account in the first line of the caption.
  • Never crop, filter or recolour an artwork. If it doesn't fit, use the Cream template.

Caption

  • First line does the work; people only see that much.
  • Plain, warm, specific. Say what it is, when, and how much.
  • Up to five hashtags, at the end. Alt text on every image.

12 · Before you post or print

Ten second check

  • The logo is a placed file, not typed text, and it has its clear space.
  • Colours are one of the approved pairs. No Canva defaults.
  • Only Inter Tight and Libre Baskerville. The logo is a placed file.
  • One big thing on the slide. Corners hold the rest. No decorations.
  • Text sits inside the safe zone for this format.
  • Every artwork is uncropped, unfiltered and credited, and the artist is tagged.
  • Alt text is written. Dates, prices and links are checked twice.
