01 · The logo

One name, five shapes

Every version is drawn from the real fonts: Faculty Glyphic for arts and hearts, Baskerville Italic for to, Nord for PROJECT. Always place the files. Never type the name out in a text box, because live text shifts the letters.

06 · Type

Four voices, each with a job

Headlines in Faculty Glyphic, the same face as arts and hearts, so every post rhymes with the logo. Labels in Nord, the face of PROJECT. Quotes and one italic phrase in Libre Baskerville, the face of to. Everything small and every number in Inter.

Do

  • Let the headline be the picture. Big, few words, plenty of ground.
  • Keep numbers in Inter. Nord draws 1 and 0 like the letters I and O.
  • Upload Faculty Glyphic, Libre Baskerville, Nord and Inter to the Canva brand kit so nobody falls back to a default.

Don't

  • Use more than three of the four in one post. Nord labels don't count.
  • Add any other font, or Canva's default.
  • Set big figures in Faculty Glyphic. Its zero has a diamond inside.
  • Centre headlines. Only the swipe pill sits in the centre.
  • Use Nord for sentences or lowercase. It only has capitals.

07 · Social

The feed looks like this

Deep Night ground, one big Rose headline set close to the top left, a round arrow badge to open, a pill at the bottom to act. Simple enough to make in five minutes, distinct enough to spot in a scroll.

Dates, prices and titles above are examples. Templates live in the Canva file.

The two moving parts

Arrow badge

  • A Rose circle with a Night arrow, as tall as the headline's capitals, placed before the first word.
  • Only on covers and posters, once per piece.
  • Points right. It means "there is more".

Pill

  • Rose fill, Night text in Inter Medium, fully rounded.
  • Two to four words that say what to do: "Swipe to find out", "Save a seat", "Shop the book".
  • One per post, at the bottom. Pills are for social and print only, never buttons on the website.

Grid rhythm

Night carries most of the grid. Break it every third post with Rose, Cream or Chilli so the profile reads as a pattern, not a wall.

Numbers

Let the figure be the picture

Figures set in Inter Medium, very large and tightly spaced, with the unit smaller beside them. One line under each says what it counts. Always give the source and date.

Motion

Reels and animated stories: text arrives with a short rise and fade (about 0.25 s), cuts land on the beat, the arrow badge can turn once. End on a 2 second card: AtH or the name on Night, one line, the web address. No spinning, bouncing or glitter effects.

09 · Captions and credits

Artists first, every time

Credit line

  • Artist Name, Title, year · medium · size.
  • Tag the artist's account in the first line of the caption.
  • Never crop, filter or recolour an artwork. If it doesn't fit, use the Cream template.

Caption

  • First line does the work; people only see that much.
  • Plain, warm, specific. Say what it is, when, and how much.
  • Up to five hashtags, at the end. Alt text on every image.

12 · Before you post or print

Ten second check

  • The logo is a placed file, not typed text, and it has its clear space.
  • Colours are one of the approved pairs. No Canva defaults.
  • Only Faculty Glyphic, Libre Baskerville, Nord and Inter. Numbers in Inter.
  • One headline, one pill, at most one badge.
  • Text sits inside the safe zone for this format.
  • Every artwork is uncropped, unfiltered and credited, and the artist is tagged.
  • Alt text is written. Dates, prices and links are checked twice.
