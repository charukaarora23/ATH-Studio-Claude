Ready to paste

$500 for one artist. Every month. No questions asked.

Introducing the ATH Creative Fund. Most funding asks artists to prove they deserve it. So we are writing the cheque. Each month, one artist receives $500 USD to spend on whatever their practice needs: materials, rent, a course, time. Free to enter.

Save this so you don't miss a step, and send it to the artist whose name belongs on this cheque.

The ATH Creative Fund is run by Arts to Hearts Project. It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram. Full terms on the application page.

06 · The numbers

What 3,000 saves takes

Saves are a share of the people who see the post, so the target is really a reach target.

If the save rate isAccounts reached for 3,000 saves
1%about 300,000
2%about 150,000
3%about 100,000

Save rates of 1 to 3% for strong, useful carousels are commonly reported by social media tools, but Instagram does not publish benchmarks. Check ATH's own past carousels in Insights for a real baseline. To reach that many accounts, the post needs help beyond the feed: collaborators, stories, partners, entries, and probably a paid boost.

07 · How Instagram works now

What the algorithm rewards

  • Sends count most for new reach. Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has named sends per reach as one of the top ranking signals. Slide 10 asks for exactly that.
  • Saves signal lasting value. Reference content (steps, dates, checklists) gets saved. That is why slides 5 to 9 exist.
  • Carousels hold attention. Each slide is another chance to save or send. Keep to 10 slides if you schedule posts through a tool.
  • The first line of the caption and the cover do the work. Most people decide in the feed.

Sources: eclincher, Sprout Social, Blck Alpaca, Metricool, True Future Media. Exact weightings in these articles are not confirmed by Instagram.

10 · Instagram's promotion rules

Two changes to the steps

  • Mention, not image tag. Instagram's promotion rules forbid encouraging people to tag content inaccurately. Asking artists to tag @artstoheartsproject on an image of their own work is close to that line. The carousel asks for a mention in the caption instead. Update step 2 on the website to match.
  • The Instagram release. Every promotion must state that it is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, and include a release of Instagram by entrants. The caption carries the statement; the full terms on the website need both.
  • Official rules. Publish eligibility, how the artist is chosen, dates, how payment is made, and any countries or ages excluded. Have them checked by a lawyer for the countries you accept.

Source: Instagram Help Center: promotion guidelines.

11 · To decide

Before posting

  • Confirm it is free to enter. The cover and caption say so; it is the strongest line in the carousel and your best answer to "pay to enter, pay for the book".
  • When the artist is chosen and announced (slide 8 says TBC).
  • Who can apply: countries, minimum age (18+ is usual).
  • How the artist is chosen: by whom, on what basis.
  • How the $500 is paid (PayPal, bank transfer, Wise).
  • Whether posts made before the launch date count.
  • Change "tag us on the image" to "mention us in the caption" on the website.
