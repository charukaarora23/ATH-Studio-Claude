01 · Interface principles

A house, not a dashboard

ATH is one house with rooms: gallery, studio, bookshop, café, reading room, open calls. Every room shares the same menu, side drawers and footer. Product screens are calmer than social: Cream and Burgundy lead, Night appears in bands and the night theme.

Do

  • One message per screen, with lots of space around it.
  • Real artwork and real words. No stock photos, no filler text.
  • Movement that feels physical: lift, settle, turn a page.
  • Sound off until the visitor turns it on.

Don't

  • Pill-shaped buttons. Buttons have 12 px corners; pills belong to social.
  • Chilli for anything but one small accent per screen.
  • Rose as text on light grounds (1.7 : 1).
  • Cards inside cards, or a shadow on everything.

08 · Accessibility

Readable by everyone

  • Text meets 4.5 : 1, large text and icons 3 : 1. Every pair on this page is measured.
  • Every control works by keyboard, with a visible 2 px accent focus ring.
  • Tap targets at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Alt text on every artwork. Artists are asked for it when they upload.
  • Voice notes have transcripts. Nothing plays on its own.
  • Errors are announced to screen readers and written in words.

11 · Logo on paper

Use the PDFs

  • Place the vector PDFs from design/logo/pdf. Never a PNG or a screenshot.
  • Minimum width: 35 mm with PROJECT, 20 mm for the name, 8 mm for AtH.
  • Foil, emboss, deboss and screen print: one-colour file only, at least 30 mm wide. Fine details in to fill in below that.
  • Reversed on Night: let the printer know, so they can choke the pink slightly to stop it filling in.

12 · Printer handoff

What to send

  • PDF/X-4 (or PDF/X-1a if the printer asks), CMYK, with bleed and crop marks.
  • Images at 300 dpi at final size; artworks colour-checked against the artist's original file.
  • Fonts embedded. Nord and Faculty Glyphic licences cover the print run.
  • A note listing the brand colours used and which need a spot match.
  • A hard proof for Night, Rose, Burgundy and Chilli before the full run.
  • Paper: uncoated cream for books to read, silk for image-heavy editions and posters.
