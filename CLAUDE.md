# ATH Studio · project memory (read first)

Arts to Hearts Project (ATH) publishes ~3 art books/magazines a month (100+ artists each), runs 2–3 open calls a month, and posts on Instagram @artstoheartsproject. **ATH Post Studio** is our in-house tool that turns calls, articles and books into on-brand Instagram carousels, stories and captions.

- App: `site/post-studio/` (static HTML/JS + Vercel serverless `api/`). Live: https://ath-post-studio.vercel.app

- Tabs (segbar): **Open calls** (`index.html` + `month.js`), **Artist interviews** (`interview.js`), **ATH Editorial** (`editorial.js`), **The Heart List** (`heartlist.js`). Daily post templates: `daily.js`.

## Brand + design rules (the user is strict about these)
- Colours: White, Burgundy/Maroon `#6A0620`, Pink `#FAD2E1`, Rose `#FFA1C2`, Ink `#1C1310`. No yellow, no gradients. Burgundy = accents and labels, never the headline colour on white.
- Fonts: Inter Tight (headlines/body, 'ATH Tight'), Libre Baskerville (quotes/paragraphs, italic for one phrase/titles; **never in capitals**), Hanken Grotesk for the brand line `.cn` "ARTS TO HEARTS PROJECT" (top-left, identical everywhere).
- ATH monogram only on cover/first slide (top-right) and end card (top centre). White on photos.
- **Artworks are never cropped** (except a full-bleed cover). Artworks big — "artworks in focus".
- **Every image carries a credit** (title by artist @handle). Auto from Heart List/interview/editorial data; otherwise the plain "Credit" button (credits are optional: no warning or label when missing); open-call cover uses the "Cover credit" field.
- No AI markers: no "01/07" counters, no section labels like "The mistake we see most / Start here", no listicle voice. Human, warm, plain English.
- Don't reinvent the user's words. Use exactly the title/copy they give (e.g. Heart List cover: "Top 5 Artists / On our Heart List / This week").
- Don't add unrequested details (open-call blurbs, extra labels). Keep it clean and consistent across tabs.

## What each tab does now
- **Open calls**: 15 feed posts, all carousels of 6+ slides (no thin countdown posts, no pushy lines); countdowns and one-slide reminders are optional **Single graphics** (`singlePosts()` in month.js, own group in the Post menu); stories, reels; styles gallery/award/editorial; credits; drag-to-move text; click text to edit; click/drop image to replace.
- Article AI (`api/article.js`) runs on Sonnet 5.5, max 6000 tokens.
- **Interviews**: carousel shows every artwork plus two cut-off answers.
- **Interviews / Editorial / Heart List**: paste an article (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C on the page, Ctrl+V into the box) — primary import; PDF upload and link import are fallbacks. Pictures save to Blob in the background. Paste again updates the open post.
- **Editorial**: carousel = cover hook → mistake (struck habit) → 2–4 tips in full (always leaves 1–3 for the article) → "the rest is in the article" (Pink highlights) → end card, keyword ARTICLE.
- **Heart List**: poster cover (artwork full-bleed or solid Maroon; own cover picture) → per artist: big artwork slide + Pink-highlight "about the artist" slide → "Tag your favourite artist" → end card (keyword HEART, "Check out our open calls. Link in bio."). Parser reads "1. Title by Artist @handle" and "01. Title — Artist @handle".
- Downloads render with `vendor/html-to-image.js` (html2canvas only as fallback); `freezeLines()` locks line breaks to the preview.
- Edits (text, moves, hidden slides, image swaps) on article posts are saved on the post (`p.edits`), shared with the team.

## The larger picture: one book's life (from the user, Nov 2026)
Goal: elevate the artists AND sell the book, professionally, with no fluff or overcrowding. Every post has one clear action.
1. **Open call (~30 days).** First half: what the book is, who the juror/curator is, what we look for, the value to an artist's life. Second half: urgency + clear action items. Valuable throughout.
2. **Jury (~10–15 days after close).** No open-call urgency. ~500 applications → ~100 selected, rest declined (dashboard notifications).
3. **Selected artists announced.** Dashboard + website + an official social post. Every selected artist is promised a carousel feature; each feature also sells the book.
4. **Book being made (~30–40 days).** Book form filled by artists; team adds curator interview, invited galleries/curators/collectors/artists, brand mission, team editorials; some artists replaced. Content: teasers while it's made (not done today, wanted), cover artist reveal, artist features. Near finalisation → **pre-order opens on Shopify**.
5. **Print (~25 days + QC + shipping).** Pre-order push: why buy, who it's for, artists' subsidised rate, friends & family discount, newsletter.
6. **Arrival + 1–2 months.** Keep promoting; artists keep promoting (share kits).
7. **Backlist.** Taper, focus on the next pre-launch, keep rotating older editions.
Books Data columns added Nov 2026 (AH–AO, read by the Sync): Call Opens, Call Closes, Juror / Curator, Selected Announced, Pre-order Opens, To Print, Ships, Friends & Family Code (dates as d mmm yyyy), AP Codes Live in Shopify (checkbox). Discount codes: the Sync fills empty Artist Disc. Code (T) + Friends & Family Code for any book with a date, in the team's style (TEXTURE26 / TEXTURE26FAM, MAG15, SVB7); never overwrites; tech team recreates in Shopify and ticks AP; posts/kits show codes only when ticked.
