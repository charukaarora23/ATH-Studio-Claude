# Print and books

## Book system
| Series | Cover ground | Accent | Trim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 Art Book (Dog, Faces…) | `burgundy-500` paint or cover artwork | `peony` | 8.5 × 8.5 in |
| The Big Book of … Artists | `sky-300` paint | `burgundy-500` | 8.5 × 8.5 in |
| ATH Catalog | `paint-garden` (petrol) | `lemon` | 8.5 × 8.5 in |
| Arts to Hearts Magazine | `peony` paint | `red-500` sticker | 8.5 × 11 in |
| Fine Art Prize catalogue | `cream-100` + winning work | `red-500` sticker | 8.5 × 11 in |

Front cover layout: see `BookCover`. Spine: title in Archivo Black, series in Inter caps, logo badge at the foot; same positions on every title so the shelf reads as one family.

## Interior
- Artist pages: artwork top 70%, credit in `caption` below, artist name in Instrument Serif italic 18pt, bio in Proxima Nova 9.5 / 14pt.
- Section openers: Archivo Black 60pt + Instrument Serif italic 40pt on a paint field.
- Margins: 0.5 in outer, 0.75 in gutter. Folios Inter 8pt, bottom outer corner.
- Paper: uncoated cream for text-led books; silk for image-heavy editions.

## Print production
- Colour: CMYK, values from the Colour section; proof `burgundy-500` and `red-500` every run (they shift most).
- Images: 300 dpi at final size. Rich black (40/30/30/100) only for large areas; text in 100K or `ink-900` CMYK.
- Export PDF/X-1a, 0.125 in bleed, crop marks.

## Other print
Certificates (`Certificate`), posters (`EventPoster`), postcards (A6, a stamp on cream), bookmarks (2 × 6 in, tape band at top), tote bags (one colour: burgundy on natural cotton, large Archivo Black word + Instrument Serif line).
