# Brand architecture

ATH is a **branded house**: every product carries the Arts to Hearts Project name and this system. Sub-brands change their name line and their lead colour, never the type, elements or voice.

| Sub-brand | Name line | Lead colour | Signature element |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arts to Hearts Project (master) | Arts to Hearts Project | `burgundy-500` | Tape |
| Open Calls | Open call · [Book name] | `burgundy-500` + `red-500` pop | Sticker (fee) |
| Books (101 Art Book, Big Book, Catalog) | [Series name] | Series colour (see Print) | Book spine |
| Arts to Hearts Magazine | Arts to Hearts Magazine · Issue [n] | `peony` | Stamp (issue number) |
| Arts to Hearts Fine Art Prize | Arts to Hearts Fine Art Prize [year] | `petrol` + `lemon` | Sticker (Win) |
| Arts to Hearts Podcast | Arts to Hearts Podcast · Ep. [n] | `sky-300` | Polaroid (guest) |
| ATH Shop | Arts to Hearts Shop | `cream-100` + `burgundy-500` | Sticker (price) |
| Artist Dashboard (apply site) | Arts to Hearts · Artist Dashboard | Semantic UI tokens | Folder tabs |

## Rules
- Always lead with "Arts to Hearts" in full on first mention; "ATH" is fine afterwards and internally.
- Sub-brand names are set in Inter `overline` above the headline, never as a separate logo.
- Partner co-branding: partner logo right, ATH badge left, divided by a 1px `ink-700` rule, equal optical size.
